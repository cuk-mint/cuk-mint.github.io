## Build language models we can actually trust — with us

MINT Lab welcomes inquiries from prospective graduate students, undergraduate
research interns, and research collaborators interested in **large language
models, retrieval-augmented generation, LLM evaluation and trustworthiness,
and LLM agents**.

We value curiosity, initiative, careful experimentation, clear communication,
and respect for collaborators.

## Opportunities

### Graduate Students (M.S. / Integrated M.S.–Ph.D.)

We invite prospective graduate students interested in LLMs, RAG, evaluation
and trustworthiness, or LLM agents. When you reach out, briefly introduce
your interests and background — we read every email.

### Undergraduate Research Interns

Undergraduate students are welcome to inquire about research internships at
any time of the year. Prior research experience is helpful but **not
required** — we value initiative, solid programming foundations, and the
willingness to read papers and test ideas carefully.

### Collaboration & Projects

We welcome research collaboration and funded projects with academic and
industry partners whose goals align with the lab's direction — LLM training
and evaluation, RAG systems, and domain-specific language models.

## Getting in touch

Email [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}). There is no fixed format,
but three things are especially useful:

- a brief self-introduction
- your research interests and motivation
- a CV

Transcripts, code samples or GitHub links, and your available start date are
welcome additions — none are required.

> An inquiry is not an offer of admission or employment. Official university
> admission requirements apply separately.
